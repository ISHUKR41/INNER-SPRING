Purpose:
The home page is the first impression and acts as a central hub for students to access mental health support. It should feel welcoming, safe, calming, and professional, while clearly guiding students to all available features.

Header (Top Section)

Left Side: Logo – a calming, trust-building icon such as a brain, lotus, or heart with soft colors (blue, green, pastel purple).

Right Side: Navigation Menu (horizontal):

Home

AI Chatbot

Appointments

Self-Assessment

Resources

Peer Support

Emergency Help

Dashboard (student view only)

About Us

Optional: Profile icon (top-right) for personalized experience (self-assessment history, upcoming appointments).

Hero Section (Above-the-Fold)

Large full-width banner with calming animation or high-quality image of students feeling relaxed, happy, or supported.

Tagline (centered): “Your mental health matters. Support is just a click away.”

CTA Buttons (prominent, center or right):

“Start Chat with AI” → AI Chatbot page

“Take Self-Assessment” → Self-Assessment page

Supporting sentence: “Confidential, anonymous, and free mental health support for all students.”

Main Body Sections

Quick Access Tiles (Central Grid or Card Layout)

Each tile includes:

Icon

Title

One-line description

Hover effect (color change, slight zoom, shadow)

Tiles to include:

AI Chatbot: “Instant support and coping strategies.”

Appointments: “Book confidential sessions with a counsellor.”

Self-Assessment: “Check your mental health with standard tools.”

Resources: “Access videos, audios, and guides.”

Peer Support: “Share and connect with other students safely.”

Emergency Help: “Immediate support during crises.”

Featured Resources / Daily Mental Health Tips (Right Side or Below Tiles)

Rotating or static section showing:

Quick tip: “Take a 5-minute breathing break.”

Featured video/audio guide with play button

Encourage regular engagement with micro-learning content.

Announcements / Campus News (Optional)

Carousel or scrollable section showing workshops, webinars, new resources, or events related to mental health.

Footer (Bottom Section)

Quick Links: Duplicate of header navigation for easy access.

Contact Info & Helplines: Phone numbers, email, emergency lines.

Disclaimer: Confidentiality and data protection notice.

Social Media / App Links: Optional for campus app downloads.

Design & UX Recommendations:

Color Palette: Soft blues (#a3cef1), greens (#a8e6cf), lavender (#d8b4e2), pastel yellow accents.

Mobile Responsiveness: Ensure tiles stack vertically on small screens.

Accessibility: Alt-text for images, readable font size, high contrast for text.

Purpose:
Provide instant, confidential, empathetic support. It should feel human-like, safe, and offer coping strategies while guiding students to resources or appointments if needed.

Layout & Structure:

Chat Interface (Full-Screen or Split-Screen)

Left Panel: Conversation history (scrollable). Can be minimized for mobile view.

Right Panel (Main Chat Window):

AI avatar or icon

Chat messages in bubbles: AI (soft blue), Student (soft gray)

Typing indicator: “AI is typing…”

Bottom Input Bar:

Text input box

Send button

Optional quick prompts buttons: “I feel anxious,” “I can’t sleep,” “I’m stressed.”

Optional voice input for accessibility

AI Chat Features:

Instant coping strategies: breathing exercises, mindfulness exercises, affirmations.

Escalation suggestions: “Would you like to book a session with a counsellor?”

Links to resources and self-assessment for guidance.

Design & UX Notes:

Friendly, supportive, and non-judgmental tone.

Use soft, calm colors to reduce stress.

Maintain privacy and anonymity.

Mobile-friendly: chat input always visible, conversation scrollable.

Purpose:
Allow students to confidentially book appointments with on-campus counsellors or mental health support services.

Optional reminders (SMS/email) without exposing sensitive info.

Clean, minimal, intuitive interface with clear CTAs.

4️⃣ Self-Assessment Page (🧠)

Purpose:
Enable students to assess their mental health status using validated tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, GHQ).

Layout & Structure:

Step-by-Step Form:

One question per screen or 2–3 grouped.

Progress bar at top (showing % completed).

Friendly, non-judgmental language: “How often have you felt anxious over the past 2 weeks?”

Score Calculation & Feedback:

Green: Normal

Orange: Moderate concern

Red: High concern

Suggestions:

AI Chatbot guidance

Resource recommendations

Book appointment

Optional Features:

Regional language support

Save results for personal tracking (anonymously)

Graphical representation: line/bar chart of previous assessments

UX Notes:

Keep it simple, non-intimidating.

Color-coded results enhance clarity.

Avoid long scrolling forms; one question at a time is best.
